WHAT THIS DOES:
A local-first, open-source alternative to ElevenLabs for instant voice cloning, zero-cost AI speech synthesis, multi-speaker audiobooks, video dubbing, and Whisper transcription. Runs offline on your GPU/CPU with built-in MCP server and OpenAI-compatible /v1 endpoints.

REAL EXAMPLE:
You have a short reference audio clip and say:
"/voicestudio Clone the speaker from voice_sample.wav and generate high-fidelity narration for script.txt using the local model."
