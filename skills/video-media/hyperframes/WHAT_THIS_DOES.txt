WHAT THIS DOES:
Enables your AI agent to create real MP4/WebM videos simply by writing standard HTML, CSS, and GSAP animations, rendering them frame-by-frame with 100% pixel-perfect consistency.

REAL EXAMPLE:
You say: "Create a 15-second product launch video announcing our new feature."
The agent writes standard HTML/CSS cards, and HyperFrames compiles it into an exportable video file with smooth easing and typography.
