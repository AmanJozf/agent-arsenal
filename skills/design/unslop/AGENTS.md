# Unslop: Anti-AI UI Redline Engine

> Universal instructions for AI coding assistants (Claude Code, Cursor, Antigravity, Codex, Windsurf, OpenCode) to de-slop interfaces and turn generic AI-generated screens into high-craft products.
> Created by Damir Sokolovsky ([@heydamir](https://x.com/heydamir)) · Licensed under CC-BY-4.0.

---

## 🎯 The Core Directive: Remove AI Slop, Elevate Product Craft

Vibecoded UI consistently fails with the same formulaic tells:
- Purple/violet gradient blobs & backgrounds
- Neon glowing borders & gradient button text
- Meaningless emoji labels (🚀 Launch, ✨ Magic, ⚡ Instant)
- "New ✨" and "Beta" badges scattered without purpose
- Uppercase tracked small labels (`FEATURE OVERVIEW`)
- Stacked cards-inside-cards with identical padding
- Shouting, generic marketing copy (*"Supercharge your workflow with next-gen AI"*)

**Unslopping fixes the craft and keeps the product:**
1. **One Bright Element:** The screen has exactly one visual hero that carries color and craft (a rendered object, painted scene, or rich chart). Everything else is quiet.
2. **Quiet Chrome:** Navigation, buttons, and panels use calm neutrals and one accent. No glows, no competing cards.
3. **Calm Space for Copy:** Headlines sit on clear negative space, short and specific, with at most one primary and one secondary action.
4. **Authentic Materials:** Real app previews with plausible data; real lighting and shadow.

---

## 🔄 The 9-Pass Redline Sequence

Execute the 9 passes in strict order across the whole screen:

1. **Spacing & Rhythm:** Fix outer margins, inner padding, and section gutters. Let layouts breathe.
2. **Color Palette:** Eliminate gratuitous gradients. Limit to 1 dominant tinted neutral canvas + 1 deliberate accent.
3. **Icons & Badges:** Strip gratuitous emojis and "✨" markers. Use functional, unified stroke icons.
4. **Copy & Voice:** Replace generic AI hype with short, direct, plain-English product claims.
5. **Typography:** Establish an intentional hierarchy; eliminate unnecessary uppercase tracked labels.
6. **Layout & Alignment:** Simplify nested card grids; align baselines and optical weights.
7. **Details & Borders:** Replace heavy borders with subtle 1px hairline dividers; soften or remove harsh drop shadows.
8. **Visuals & Art Direction:** Anchor the screen with one memorable hero asset or realistic product preview.
9. **Motion & Feedback:** Keep micro-interactions fast (150–200ms) and quiet; eliminate infinite pulsing animations.

## ⚡ Direct Prompt Template

```text
Audit and unslop this page. Follow the 9-pass Unslop redline rules:
remove AI tells (purple gradients, glows, emojis, nested cards), quiet the chrome,
and give this product a distinct, human-crafted design direction.
```
