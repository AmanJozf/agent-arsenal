WHAT THIS DOES:
The viral image-to-3D procedural reconstruction skill (21k+ stars). It turns any 2D reference photo of an object into an interactive, code-only Three.js 3D model with realistic lighting, PBR materials, and orbit controls—no external 3D files needed.

REAL EXAMPLE:
You provide a photo of a retro camera or sneaker and say:
"Reconstruct this camera as an interactive 3D Three.js model."
The agent inspects the photo, writes procedural 3D geometries and shaders in Three.js, and renders an interactive 3D model you can rotate and zoom in your browser.
