# Academic Research Agent: Graduate & PhD Research Guide

Human-in-the-loop technical research skill tailored for Master's and PhD work in Computer Science, AI, and Engineering (origin: `ngtiendong/Academic-Research-Agent-Skill`).

## Core Principles
1. **Human Researcher Ownership:** The researcher owns hypotheses, direction, and final claims.
2. **Research Reality Gates:** Before writing or running heavy experiments, verify data access, compute feasibility, and metric alignment.
3. **Formal Mathematical Scoping:** State technical problems with formal mathematical rigor, inputs, outputs, mapping functions, and falsifiable claims.
4. **Traceable Chains:** Keep a strict link between research question -> contribution -> formal claim -> baseline competitor -> evaluation result.

## Workflow Triggers
- **Scope & Problem Formulation:** Formalize the thesis objective and research questions.
- **Novelty Review:** Benchmark against closest prior art to isolate true residual deltas.
- **Reality Gate:** Audit whether proposed claims can actually be supported by available code and data.
- **Experiment Planning:** Formulate clean benchmarks, metrics, and ablation studies.
