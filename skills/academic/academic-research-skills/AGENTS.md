# Academic Research Skills: Agent Operational Guide

Comprehensive multi-agent suite for academic research, thesis development, paper drafting, and rigorous peer review.

## Sub-Skills Overview

| Skill | Path | Primary Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Deep Research** | [`deep-research/SKILL.md`](./deep-research/SKILL.md) | 13-agent literature survey, evidence synthesis, PRISMA reviews, and reference verification. |
| **Academic Paper** | [`academic-paper/SKILL.md`](./academic-paper/SKILL.md) | Outlining, drafting, structuring, and revising research papers and thesis chapters. |
| **Academic Paper Reviewer** | [`academic-paper-reviewer/SKILL.md`](./academic-paper-reviewer/SKILL.md) | Simulated peer-review, rubric assessment, identifying unsubstantiated claims and citation gaps. |
| **Academic Pipeline** | [`academic-pipeline/SKILL.md`](./academic-pipeline/SKILL.md) | 10-stage end-to-end research lifecycle orchestrator with human verification gates. |
| **SR Screener** | [`sr-screener/SKILL.md`](./sr-screener/SKILL.md) | Title/abstract and full-text screening for systematic literature reviews. |

## Operational Rules
1. **Evidence-Grounded Writing:** Never invent references or hallucinate citations. Flag missing citations explicitly as `[CITATION NEEDED]`.
2. **Clear Separation of Concerns:**
   - Use `deep-research` for literature exploration and fact-checking.
   - Use `academic-paper` for drafting methodology, architecture, and evaluations.
   - Use `academic-paper-reviewer` for quality control and pre-submission audits.
3. **Anti-Slop Standard:** Keep prose concise, scholarly, objective, and devoid of generic AI conversational filler.
